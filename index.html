Tawakul

Prayer times and reminders for wherever you are, with an optional App Lock that holds your most distracting apps until you've prayed.

Support: email ahmedsalahheidar@gmail.com and we'll get back to you within two business days.

Tawakul is a personal reminder tool. Prayer times are calculated estimates; follow your local mosque when they differ.

Common questions

My prayer times don't match my mosque

Open Settings and choose the calculation method and Asr setting your mosque uses. "Automatic" picks the method most used in your region.

App Lock didn't block my apps

Check that App Lock shows Active on the Home screen, that Screen Time permission is still granted, and that you've selected at least one app or category. If it still happens, email us the date and prayer it missed and we'll help.

How do I unlock my apps?

Open Tawakul and tap I prayed on the prayer. You can also turn App Lock off at any time in Settings.

How do I delete my account?

An account is optional. If you created one, sign in and go to Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account.
