What the app is

Tawakul helps you keep your daily prayers with prayer time reminders, an optional App Lock that temporarily blocks apps you choose, and daily Islamic content. It is a supportive tool, not a substitute for your own responsibility in observing prayer, and not a source of religious rulings.

Prayer times

Prayer times are calculated from your location and the calculation method you choose, using a third-party service. We aim for accuracy but cannot guarantee that times will always match your local mosque or authorities. When they differ, follow your mosque.

App Lock

App Lock relies on Apple's Screen Time features. iOS decides when blocking takes effect, so it may occasionally start late or not apply. You can always open Tawakul to unlock your apps, and turn App Lock off in Settings. Do not rely on App Lock for anything safety-critical.
